# Message Autopsy: The Buyer Said What?

**Spec campaign by Hatim Abuasabeh**  
**Built:** September 11, 2026  
**Status:** Concept / not yet launched

> This is spec work. The output below is real; performance metrics would only be reported after a live test.

## The problem

B2B landing pages often sound polished but interchangeable. AI makes it even easier to produce clean copy that says very little. A VP of Product Marketing does not need another generic checklist. They need a way to see exactly where buyer interpretation diverges from the message the company intended.

## Campaign idea

**Message Autopsy: The Buyer Said What?**

A recurring content series that takes one B2B claim, shows what the company probably intended, then contrasts it with the questions a real buyer would ask.

The recurring format:

1. **The claim**
2. **What the company means**
3. **What a skeptical VP hears**
4. **What evidence is missing**
5. **A sharper rewrite**
6. **How to test it with buyers**

## Example episode

### The claim

"AI-powered insights that help your team move faster."

### What a skeptical VP hears

"Another platform with AI in the headline. Faster at what? Compared with what? Which decision gets easier?"

### Sharper rewrite

"Test your message with verified B2B buyers in 48 hours, so your team can fix the weak claim before paid traffic sees it."

### CTA

**Think your homepage says what buyers hear? Test it.**

## Three-post sequence

**Post 1: The autopsy**  
Show the claim, the likely buyer reaction, and one rewrite.

**Post 2: The disagreement**  
Ask PMMs which line they would keep, kill, or test. Use comments as qualitative signal.

**Post 3: The proof**  
Show what evidence would settle the debate and connect it to a real message test.

## Three-email sequence

**Email 1 - Subject:** Your homepage may be clear to everyone except the buyer  
Introduce the series with one before/after example.

**Email 2 - Subject:** The sentence your team is defending too hard  
Explain why internal familiarity hides ambiguity.

**Email 3 - Subject:** Put the argument in front of the buyer  
Invite the reader to test the live page or campaign message.

## Why it can earn attention

The format is visual, opinionated, repeatable, and native to what senior PMMs already do: review positioning, defend copy, and resolve stakeholder disagreement. It gives Wynter a useful point of view without hiding the product behind generic thought leadership.

## Measurement

Primary metric: qualified demos created by the series.

Secondary metrics: saves, shares, comments from PMM titles, click-through to message-test pages, assisted conversions, and demo conversion by episode topic.
